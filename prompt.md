# Install SweepMyTV on my Android TV

Guide me through installing the official SweepMyTV preview on a TV I own. Start by asking for my TV brand and model, Android version, and whether I’m using a coding assistant with a terminal on my computer. In chat-only mode, give me one step at a time and wait for my result.

1. Start on the TV. Help me find the build entry under **Settings → About** and press OK on it seven times to unlock **Developer options**. Have me enable **Wireless debugging** or the manufacturer’s **Network debugging** option and put the TV and computer on the same Wi-Fi. Confirm the TV runs Android TV or Google TV with Android 8.0 or later and supports APK installation. If debugging is unavailable, point me to https://sweepmytv.com/tutorials/android-phone/.
2. Use Android Platform Tools from https://developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools. Get the preview version, download path, and SHA-256 from https://sweepmytv.com/release.json. Use the signed APK at that path under https://sweepmytv.com/downloads/ and verify its SHA-256 before installing. Stop if the download or checksum verification fails.
3. Connect only to the TV address I provide. If the TV has **Pair device with pairing code**, use its pairing address with `adb pair` and let me enter the code locally when prompted. Then use the connection address on the main Wireless debugging screen with `adb connect`. If the TV offers network debugging without pairing, use the address it provides and have me approve the connection on the TV. Use `adb devices -l` to confirm the target is authorized and confirm its model before installing.
4. Use `adb -s <confirmed TV address or serial> install -r <verified APK path>` for the package `com.sweepmytv.launcher`. Preserve existing app data on an update. If the TV reports a signature conflict or blocks the install, stop and explain the exact error. Keep the original launcher and my other apps installed.
5. Have me open **Sweep TV** from the TV’s Apps screen. Ask me to confirm that it opens and that I can navigate the dock with the remote. If I want it to open when I press Home, guide me through the TV’s Home app setting and let me choose. Keep permissions, package cleanup, remote mappings, and system settings outside the install scope.
6. Report the version and the checks we actually completed. Disconnect the ADB session when finished and show me where to turn debugging off on the TV.
